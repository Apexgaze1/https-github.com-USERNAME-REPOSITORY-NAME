BUSINESS RESOURCES

Practical checklists for organizing business work.

Simple starting points for reviewing workflows, cleaning business information and preparing useful research.

01

Workflow review checklist

Use these questions when a recurring process feels slow, confusing or inconsistent.

  • What starts the process?
  • Who performs each step?
  • What information or files are needed?
  • Where does work wait or get repeated?
  • What does the finished result look like?
02

Spreadsheet cleanup checklist

A basic checklist for making business spreadsheets easier to maintain and use.

  • Remove duplicate records.
  • Standardize names, dates and categories.
  • Check missing or inconsistent values.
  • Separate raw data from working reports.
  • Document important columns and rules.
03

Business research brief

Before starting research, define the question so the final findings are usable.

  • State the research question.
  • Define the target market or companies.
  • Identify the information required.
  • Record sources and dates.
  • Summarize findings and next actions.
