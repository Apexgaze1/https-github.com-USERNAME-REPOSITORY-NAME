Privacy Notice

Information about website inquiries and contact-form submissions.

Information you provide

When you send Apex a web message or email, the information you provide may be used to review your inquiry, respond to you, and discuss potential services.

Contact form processing

The website contact form uses Netlify Forms to process and store submitted inquiries in the site owner’s Netlify dashboard. Netlify states that form submissions are available in the site Forms area and that email notifications can be configured for verified submissions.

Information we do not request

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, medical information, or other sensitive information through the website contact form.

Website information

Third-party hosting, email, security, and website services may process technical information needed to operate and protect the site.

Questions

Contact apexadministrative@gmail.com with privacy questions.
